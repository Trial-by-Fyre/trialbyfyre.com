NextSeason TV

Have you ever finished watching the current season of a TV show, and immediately wondered when or if the next season would be available? If so, NextSeason TV is for you.

NextSeason TV will keep track of the status of your favorite shows and notify you whenever anything changes – a new season is announced, the launch date of the first episode of the next season is revealed, or (hopefully rarely) a show ends.

NextSeason TV is currently in development for iPhone and will be available on the App Store.
